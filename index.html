Why it keeps coming back

You keep tackling the problem. The system keeps rebuilding it.

For decades, the impact economy has operated on a reductionist assumption: break the problem into parts, solve each part in isolation, measure the outputs. The results are familiar: wins dissolve and problems resurface in new forms. Well-funded interventions generate activity without structural change.

This is not a failure of effort or resources. It is a failure of approach.

Complex systems are governed by interdependencies, feedback loops, and causal architectures that remain invisible to the tools most organizations use. Intervening without a map generates unintended consequences and the recurrence of symptoms from dynamics that were never addressed at source.

shftrs maps these underlying causal relationships and finds where to intervene.

Our approach

See the system. Find the leverage.

The academically published shftrs methodology synthesizes the most rigorous elements of systems mapping and complexity analysis from leading global traditions.

Screenshot — CLD
01 — CLD

Causal Loop Diagram

A visual representation of the system a client operates within — actors, variables, feedback loops, and causal relationships constituting the real architecture of the challenge. shftrs builds CLDs using exclusively client-provided data: your reports, field notes, interviews, and research. Not a generic LLM's approximation of your context but your system, built from your evidence, and grounded in data you can defend to stakeholders.

Screenshot — LPA
02 — LPA

Leverage Point Analysis

Drawing on Donella Meadows' foundational theory — the places in a system where small interventions produce disproportionate, structural change — shftrs applies a suite of analytical lenses to identify where interventions can produce lasting change rather than symptomatic relief. Each of the 15 lenses reveals a different leverage perspective leaning on: system archetypes, performance gaps, power dynamics, system narratives, creative tensions, and many more. The combination is what produces the depth and defensibility that no single-lens analysis can achieve.

A shftrs engagement delivers interconnected outputs — not a report to file, but a working instrument for navigating what comes next.

01
Validated Causal Loop Diagram

A visual map of the system — shared, communicable, and validated through participatory rounds that build stakeholder trust before implementation begins. A universal communication medium across your organisation and its partners.

02
Multi-Lens Leverage Point Analysis

A rigorous, multi-perspective analysis identifying where within the system interventions are most likely to produce structural, lasting change — rather than the symptomatic relief that drives the recurring pattern of illusory wins.

03
Prioritised Intervention Roadmap

Not just where to intervene, but how — specifying the form of intervention most suited to each leverage point: coalition-building, policy influence, new venture creation, scaling, or combinations. Sequence and priority built in.

04
Stakeholder Analysis

A mapping of who to work with, who to neutralise, and in what form — organised by intervention logic, not organisational chart. Built from the same system map, so the connections between actors and leverage points are explicit.
