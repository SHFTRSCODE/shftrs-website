The structural failure

Linear thinking built the tools. Complex problems exposed their limits.

For decades, the impact economy has operated on a reductionist assumption: break the problem into parts, solve each part in isolation, measure the outputs. The results are familiar. Wins dissolve. Problems resurface in new forms. Well-funded interventions generate activity without structural change.

This is not a failure of effort or resources. It is a failure of epistemology. Complex systems are governed by interdependencies, feedback loops, and causal architectures that remain invisible to the tools most organisations use. Intervening without a map does not fail quietly — it generates unintended consequences and the rebound of dynamics that were never addressed at source.

shftrs maps the system. Then finds where to intervene.

The methodology

Two interconnected instruments. One coherent picture.

Developed over five years of applied practice and academically published, the shftrs methodology synthesises the most rigorous elements of systems mapping and complexity analysis from leading global traditions.

01 — CLD

Causal Loop Diagram

A visual representation of the system a client operates within — actors, variables, feedback loops, and causal relationships constituting the real architecture of the challenge. shftrs builds CLDs using exclusively client-provided data: your reports, field notes, interviews, and research. Not a generic AI model's approximation of your context. Your system, built from your evidence — grounded in data you can defend to funders and stakeholders.

02 — LPA

Leverage Point Analysis

Drawing on Donella Meadows' foundational theory — the places in a system where small interventions produce disproportionate, structural change — shftrs applies a suite of analytical lenses to identify where intervention produces lasting change rather than symptomatic relief. Six lenses are fully developed, with six to eight more in progress. Each reveals a different category of leverage. The combination is what produces the depth and defensibility that no single-lens analysis can achieve.

What you receive

A complete foundation for systemic intervention.

A shftrs engagement delivers interconnected outputs — not a report to file, but a working instrument for navigating what comes next.

01
Validated Causal Loop Diagram

A visual map of the system — shared, communicable, and validated through participatory rounds that build stakeholder trust before implementation begins. A universal communication medium across your organisation and its partners.

02
Multi-Lens Leverage Point Analysis

A rigorous, multi-perspective analysis identifying where within the system interventions are most likely to produce structural, lasting change — rather than the symptomatic relief that drives the recurring pattern of illusory wins.

03
Prioritised Intervention Roadmap

Not just where to intervene, but how — specifying the form of intervention most suited to each leverage point: coalition-building, policy influence, new venture creation, scaling, or combinations. Sequence and priority built in.

04
Stakeholder Analysis

A mapping of who to work with, who to neutralise, and in what form — organised by intervention logic, not organisational chart. Built from the same system map, so the connections between actors and leverage points are explicit.
